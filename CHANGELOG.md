# Changelog

## 0.1.0

The first release.

- Hold your iPhone next to your Mac; the orb on the phone and the light at the Mac's screen edge follow how close it is.
- Press and hold the orb to pair, compare a six-digit code, accept on the Mac.
- Throw a card off the iPhone's screen to send it; it burns into light and comes out of the Mac's screen edge.
- Drag files onto the Mac's screen edge (or the menu bar panel) to send them to the iPhone.
- Photos, videos and any file, end-to-end encrypted, straight between the devices.
- Files of 8 MB and up carry on in the background.
- The Mac app keeps itself up to date (Sparkle).
